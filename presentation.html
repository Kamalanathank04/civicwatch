Problem – Proposed Solution

Hackfor
Social Cause
Core Problem vs. Proposed Solution Architecture:
Civic Bottleneck Current Government Portals (Swachhata / Helpline) CivicWatch Innovative Solution
Citizen Onboarding Mandatory mobile OTP, phone verification, and app downloads create high drop-off. Zero-Login, Anonymous by Default: File photo reports in < 45s on any mobile browser.
Evidence & Data Heavy camera uploads often fail on 2G/3G connections or exhaust browser storage. On-Device Canvas Compression: Resizes photos to < 50KB WebP before transmission.
Location Capture Rigid GPS coordinates fail indoors or in narrow lanes, blocking submissions. Resilient Dual-Mode: Browser GPS with instant manual street/landmark fallback.
Duplicate Triage Redundant tickets for the same dump swamp municipal sanitation supervisors. Explainable Deduplication: Flags active reports within 72h; 1-click citizen corroboration.
Accountability Deficit Citizens feel complaints enter an administrative black hole with zero updates. Public Audit Timeline: Unique token (CW-ID) tracks crew dispatch & clearance notes.
Innovation Highlight: CivicWatch does not reject similar complaints; it converts potential duplicate tickets into Community Corroborations that elevate issue severity to Urgent SLA for faster municipal dispatch.
Tested Prototype Feature: Duplicate Heuristic Active
Proximity rule: Category + Street within 72h window.

Feasibility and Risk Analysis

Hackfor
Social Cause
Risk Mitigation & Technical Feasibility Assessment:
Identified Risk Risk Level Demonstrated Prototype Mitigation Production Roadmap Defense
Malicious / Spam Reports Medium / High Mandatory photo validation, minimum 15-character observation length, client-side rate limits. Edge MobileNet AI waste classification before ticket generation.
Duplicate Ticket Overload High / High Explainable Deduplication Heuristic: Auto-flags matching category & street within 72h window. PostgreSQL / PostGIS spatial radius queries (ST_DWithin < 200m).
Browser Storage Quota Medium / Medium HTML5 Canvas downsamples high-res photos to < 50KB WebP (prevents 5MB storage limit error). Offloaded to secure cloud object storage (AWS S3 / S3-compatible).
GPS Failure / Refusal High / Medium Graceful fallback to manual Ward, Street, and Landmark input fields without blocking submission. Address autocomplete via open municipal GIS boundary layers.
Privacy & Citizen Safety Low / High Zero PII collected by default: Anonymous toggle enabled; phone optional for SMS tracking. Zero external tracking scripts, cookies, or third-party analytics pixels.
Deployment Feasibility: CivicWatch requires zero app store installations, zero proprietary database licenses, and zero paid API keys, enabling immediate deployment across budget devices.
