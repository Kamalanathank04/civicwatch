## 3. Step-by-Step Portal Navigation & Submission Workflow

### Step 1: Login to MY Bharat Portal
1. Navigate to [https://mybharat.gov.in](https://mybharat.gov.in).
2. Click on **Login** in the top right corner.
3. Authenticate using your registered Mobile Number and OTP, or MeriPehchaan (National Single Sign-On).

### Step 2: Navigate to Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue (VBYLD)
1. On the user dashboard, locate the banner for **Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue (VBYLD)**.
2. Click on **Hack for Social Cause (HSC)**.
3. Verify your team dashboard shows Team ID: `HSC|TN|03258` and State: `Tamil Nadu`.

### Step 3: Project Submission Form Fields
Complete the official submission form carefully:
- **Project Title:** `CivicWatch — Citizen-Powered Cleaner Cities`
- **Theme Selection:** `Clean & Sustainable Communities`
- **Abstract / Summary:**
  > *"CivicWatch is a lightweight, privacy-preserving civic issue reporting and municipal action tracking system. By providing zero-friction anonymous reporting with photographic evidence, browser-based geolocation, unique complaint IDs (CW-2025-TN-XXXX), and an explainable duplicate detection engine, CivicWatch eliminates citizen reporting friction while shielding municipal sanitation supervisors from duplicate report fatigue."*
- **Presentation Upload:** Attach your completed 7-slide PDF/PPT deck based on `@HSC submission- Template`.
- **Source Code Repository URL:** Paste your public GitHub repository link containing the runnable codebase, automated test suite, and documentation.
- **Demo Video URL:** Paste the link to your 2–3 minute video walkthrough.
- **AI Tools Disclosed:** State: *"Google Antigravity / Gemini was used as an agentic pair-programming assistant for technical design documentation, CSS styling, test suite scripting, and refactoring."*
